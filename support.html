Before writing, check these common cases

The AI try-on returns no image

The app needs your own Google AI Studio key to generate looks. Check in Settings that the key is saved and still valid in your Google account. If the key is correct and no image comes back, the app shows the reason returned by Google on the error screen: copy that text and send it to us.

How to create the Google AI Studio key

The key is free to create and belongs to you, not to us. Go to aistudio.google.com, sign in with your Google account, open the API keys area, create a new key and paste it into Settings in KR Provador. Generation usage is billed by Google directly to you, separately from the app subscription.

The garment cutout came out wrong

Light garments need a dark background, dark garments need a light one. Open-back dresses and sheer fabrics confuse the automatic cutout: use the eraser inside the app, with zoom for the details. If it still looks wrong, retake the photo on a flat surface without hard shadows.

The AI changed my face or my body

Fill in your profile with height, weight, age and body shape: that is what makes the result respect your body. On the look screen, use the quick corrections and the notes field to say what went wrong — the next request is generated with that correction applied.

Subscription

The subscription is annual with automatic renewal, and the purchase is handled by Apple. To view, change or cancel it, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, go to Subscriptions and choose KR Provador. Cancellation must be done at least 24 hours before the period ends. If you changed devices or reinstalled the app, use Restore purchases inside the app's Settings.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving your wardrobe to another device

In Settings, use Export to create a file with everything you added. Send that file to the other device via AirDrop or the Files app and use Import there. You choose whether to replace what exists or merge it.

Deleting your data

Everything stays on your device. You can delete item by item, use Delete everything in Settings, or uninstall the app, which removes its whole folder from the device. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. An internet connection is needed only when generating a look; the rest of the app works offline.
