How the subscription works

The subscription is annual with automatic renewal and gives you 25 AI generations per month. The month counts from the day you subscribed: if you subscribed on the 12th, you get 25 new generations every 12th. In months without that day, the reset happens on the last day of the month. Unused generations do not carry over.

If you have never subscribed, you get a 7-day free trial with 5 generations. Apple allows the trial only once per Apple Account. After the 7 days, the annual charge starts. To avoid paying, cancel at least 24 hours before the 7 days end.

Your wardrobe, outfits, photos and suggestions work without a subscription. You only need it to have the AI dress you in your clothes.

Extra credits

If you run out of this month's 25 generations, you can buy packs of 10, 50 or 100 generations in Ajustes (Settings) with the Comprar créditos (Buy credits) button. The packs are shown only to people with an active subscription.

  • The app uses this month's generations first, then your credits.
  • Purchased credits never expire. If your subscription ends, the credits stay saved and work again when you subscribe again.
  • Each generation uses 1, including when you tap Gerar de novo (Generate again).
  • For safety, the limit is 15 generations per hour. If you see "Muitas gerações em pouco tempo" (Too many generations in a short time), wait a few minutes.

Your balance is shown right under the Vestir com IA (Try on with AI) button and in Settings. When it reaches zero, that button is replaced by Comprar mais gerações (Buy more generations).

A generation failed

If Google's AI returns an error, takes too long, blocks the image or answers without an image, the generation goes back to your balance automatically. You do not need to ask for anything.

If the error keeps happening, send us the message shown on screen, with the time it happened.

Restore purchases

New iPhone, or deleted and reinstalled the app? Open Settings in KR Provador and tap Restaurar compras (Restore purchases). Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Your balance is stored with the subscription, so this month's generations and your credits show up on any device signed in to that Apple Account.

The wardrobe itself lives on the device. To take it with you, see "Moving your wardrobe to another device" below.

Cancel the subscription

In the app, open Settings and tap Gerenciar assinatura (Manage subscription). Or, outside the app: iPhone Settings, tap your name, go to Subscriptions and choose KR Provador.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. After cancelling you can keep using the app until the expiry date, and any credits left stay saved.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us, at reportaproblem.apple.com. When Apple refunds a credit pack, those generations are removed from your balance. If they were already used, your credit balance goes below zero until the next purchase covers it. Your monthly subscription generations keep working.

The garment cutout came out wrong

Light garments need a dark background, dark garments need a light one. Open-back dresses and sheer fabrics confuse the automatic cutout: use the eraser inside the app, with zoom for the details. If it still looks wrong, retake the photo on a flat surface without hard shadows.

The AI changed my face or my body

Fill in Meus dados (My data) with height, weight, age and body shape: that is what makes the result respect your body. On the outfit screen, use the quick corrections and the note field to say what went wrong. The next generation takes that correction into account.

Moving your wardrobe to another device

In Settings, use Exportar guarda-roupa (Export wardrobe) to create a file with everything you added. Send that file to the other device via AirDrop or the Files app and use Importar de um arquivo (Import from a file) there. You choose whether to replace what exists or merge it.

Deleting your data

Your wardrobe stays on your device. You can delete item by item, use Apagar tudo (Delete everything) in Settings, or uninstall the app, which removes its whole folder from the device.

The server keeps only the record of purchases and generations, with no photos. To delete that record, write to krprovador@gmail.com with the order ID shown on your Apple receipt. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and being 18 or older. An internet connection is needed only when generating a look; the rest of the app works offline.
