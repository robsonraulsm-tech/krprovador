In one sentence: everything you add stays inside your iPhone or iPad, and nothing is sent to any server of ours. The only images that leave your device are the ones you choose to send to the AI, at the moment you tap Try on with AI.

Who is responsible

KR Provador is developed and maintained by Robson Raul, as an individual. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to krprovador@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device

The app saves the following inside its own sandboxed folder on your device. None of it passes through a server of ours, because we do not operate one:

  • Photos of your clothes, shoes and accessories.
  • Your full body photos, including any posed photos you add.
  • The images generated by the AI.
  • The details you enter under My data: gender, age, height, weight and body shape.
  • The outfits you build, with their name, date, notes and corrections.
  • The API key you enter in Settings.

We have no access to any of it. We cannot view, copy or recover the contents of your device.

What is sent to the AI

The virtual try-on runs on the Google Gemini image API. Data is sent only when you tap Try on with AI, never in the background and never without your action.

At that moment, the following leaves your device and goes directly to Google:

  • The body photo you selected as the base.
  • A magnified crop of your face, taken from that same photo, so the AI keeps your identity.
  • The photos of the garments in the outfit.
  • When enabled in Settings, a neutral photo of you used as a sizing reference.
  • Text containing your profile details, the pose you picked, the corrections you flagged and any notes you wrote.

The connection goes from your device straight to Google, without passing through us. We receive no copy of these images and we do not know when or how often you use the feature.

How Google handles that content is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy, which vary depending on the type of key and plan you use. We recommend reading Google's terms before sending personal photos. They are available at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

About your API key

The key you create in Google AI Studio is yours, is entered by you, and is stored only on your device. It is used solely to authenticate the requests the app makes to Google on your behalf. We never see this key, and it is not sent anywhere else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, a login, an email address or a phone number.
  • It uses no trackers, no advertising pixels and no usage analytics.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, calendar or microphone.
  • It shows no advertising.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with data brokers or commercial partners.

Camera and photo library access is requested only so you can choose the pictures you want to add, and the app reads only the images you select.

Subscription and payment

If the app offers a subscription, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple, inside the App Store purchase system. We do not receive, see or store your card number or any payment details. Apple only tells us whether your subscription is active.

Backup and transfer

The export feature creates a file containing your wardrobe, and that file is under your control. You decide where it goes, whether by AirDrop or saved into the Files app. The app does not upload it to us or to any cloud service on its own.

How long data is kept

For as long as you want. Nothing expires. You can delete garments, photos and outfits one by one, use Delete everything in Settings, or simply uninstall the app. On uninstall, iOS removes the app's entire folder and the content is permanently gone from the device. If you have not made a backup first, it cannot be recovered, not even by us.

Your rights

Brazil's LGPD and the European Union's GDPR give you rights of access, correction, deletion and portability over your data.

In practice those rights are already in your hands here: the data sits on your device, you can see all of it through the app's interface, edit whatever you want, export it as a file and delete it whenever you decide. Since we keep no copy of anything, there is no request to make to us. Even so, if you have any question about this, write to the address at the bottom of this page.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes in how the app works, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Material changes, such as sending data to a new destination, will be announced inside the app before they take effect.

Contact

Privacy questions, requests or complaints: krprovador@gmail.com.
