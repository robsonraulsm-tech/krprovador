Privacy Policy

Summary: your wardrobe, photos and outfits stay on your iPhone. Only when you tap Vestir com IA (Try on with AI) do the photos of that outfit go through the KR Provador server to Google's Gemini AI, which draws the clothes on you. The server stores no photos. It only keeps count of the generations you made and the credits you bought.

Who is responsible

KR Provador is developed and maintained by Robson Raul, as an individual. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to krprovador@gmail.com.

What stays on your device only

The app saves the following inside its own sandboxed folder on your iPhone:

  • Photos of your clothes, shoes and accessories.
  • Your full body photos, including posed photos.
  • The images generated by the AI.
  • What you fill in under Meus dados (My data): gender, age, height, weight and body shape.
  • The outfits you build, with their name, date, notes and corrections.

We have no access to any of it. We cannot view, copy or recover the contents of your device.

What leaves the device, and when

Only when you tap Vestir com IA (Try on with AI), and only after you accept the notice shown the first time. Never in the background. At that moment the app sends:

  • The body photo you picked as the base.
  • A magnified crop of your face, taken from that same photo, so the AI keeps your identity.
  • The photos of the garments in the outfit, with the name and color you gave each one.
  • Your neutral sizing photo, when the base is a posed photo and that option is on in Ajustes (Settings).
  • Whatever is filled in under My data.
  • The pose you chose, the corrections you flagged and the note you wrote for the AI.

You can turn this permission off in Settings. When it is off, the app asks for your consent again before sending any photo.

Where this information goes

The request goes from the app to the KR Provador server, which runs on Cloudflare. The server checks that you have a subscription or credit, forwards the request to Google's Gemini API and sends the finished image back to the app. The photos are held in the server's memory only while being forwarded and are never written to storage.

We use the paid tier of the Gemini API. On that tier:

  • Google does not use your photos or text to train or improve its products.
  • Google keeps requests and responses for up to 55 days, only to detect abuse and meet legal requirements. If its systems flag a request as a possible violation, authorized Google staff may review that content.

Details are in the Gemini API terms and the Gemini API usage policies. Traffic goes through Cloudflare's network, which follows the Cloudflare privacy policy.

What the server keeps

To count your balance, stop a purchase from being used twice and give back a generation that failed, the server keeps:

  • The number Apple assigns to your subscription. It is the same on every device signed in to the same Apple Account, and it is how the server knows whose balance it is.
  • The subscription status, which the server checks with Apple: whether it is active, whether it is in the free trial, the start date and the expiry date.
  • The credits you bought: which pack, how many generations, the date, the Apple transaction number and whether it was refunded.
  • Each generation: date and time, whether it used the monthly quota or a credit, and whether it succeeded or was given back.

The server does not store photos, text, names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses or device identifiers.

This record is kept while there is a subscription or a credit balance, because paid credits never expire. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time, as explained below.

Purchases

The subscription and the credits are bought inside the App Store and processed by Apple. We do not receive, see or store your card number or any payment details. Apple tells the server what was bought and when, and notifies it of renewals, cancellations and refunds.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, a login, an email address or a phone number.
  • It uses no trackers, no advertising pixels and no usage analytics.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, calendar or microphone.
  • It shows no advertising.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with data brokers or commercial partners.

Camera and photo library access is requested only so you can choose the pictures you want to add, and the app reads only the images you select.

Backup and transfer

The export feature creates a file containing your wardrobe, and that file is under your control. You decide where it goes, whether by AirDrop or saved into the Files app. The app does not upload it to us or to any cloud service on its own.

Minimum age: 18

KR Provador is for adults aged 18 and over only. This is required by Google's Gemini API terms, which do not allow the API to be used by minors or in apps directed at them or likely to be used by them. If you know of anyone under 18 using the app, write to us and we will delete the record linked to the purchase.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

On your device: for as long as you want. You can delete garments, photos and outfits one by one, use Apagar tudo (Delete everything) in Settings, or uninstall the app. On uninstall, iOS removes the app's entire folder. If you have not made a backup first, it cannot be recovered, not even by us.

On the server: write to krprovador@gmail.com asking us to delete your usage record. Include the order ID shown on your Apple receipt email so we can find the right record. We delete it within 15 days. Deleting the record resets your credit balance to zero, and if the subscription stays active, a new record starts with your next generation.

At Google: requests are deleted automatically within 55 days.

Your rights

Brazil's LGPD and the European Union's GDPR give you rights of access, correction, deletion and portability over your data.

What is on your device you control through the app itself: you can view, edit, export and delete it. For the server record, email us to ask for a copy or for deletion, and we reply within 15 days. You can also complain to the ANPD in Brazil or to the data protection authority of your EU country, which in France is the CNIL.
